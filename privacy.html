Privacy Policy — Refax Health

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Refax Health (the “app”) is a personal application used only by its owner — a single Google account — to read the owner's own Google Health and Fitbit data (sleep, sleep stages, weight, steps, heart rate, and related wellness metrics) and turn it into personal recovery and weight-loss reports.

What data is used

Only health data that already belongs to the account owner, present in the owner's Google account. It is accessed solely through the owner's own OAuth authorization of this app to that owner's own account. No data of any other person is accessed.

How it is used

Exclusively to produce the owner's personal reports. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles about anyone other than the account owner.

Storage & deletion

Reports are stored only on the owner's own device or server. You can delete this data and revoke the app's access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & with sign-in.
